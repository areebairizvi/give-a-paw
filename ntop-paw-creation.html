nTop Paw Creation¶

The paw is the part of the prosthetic that meets the ground, so its lattice must balance grip, cushioning, and durability. This page documents the Paw block: what it does, the inputs you can change, and an interactive demo of how each input reshapes the paw.

Paw Lattice¶

The Paw block builds the foot of the prosthetic from a honeycomb lattice. Below is the block as it appears in nTop.

Tip: Click any input on the block to expand its live demo. Each demo changes one input while the others stay at their defaults, so the difference you see comes from that parameter alone. Drag the model to rotate it, scroll to zoom, and use the view cube to snap or step the orientation.
